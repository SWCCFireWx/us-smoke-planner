About the numbers

Forecast fields come from the NOAA National Blend of Models (NBM): 2.5-km CONUS, 3-km Alaska, and Hawaii and Puerto Rico grids, averaged to about 10–14 km and smoothly interpolated. Ventilation rate = mixing height × transport wind. Confidence layers use NBM's ensemble spread when NBM provides it. Smoke is NOAA's HRRR model (CONUS, 48 h). Overnight settling risk is this site's screening index (low overnight mixing height and transport wind, with high RH adding fog risk); it is not LVORI. Chance of precip is NBM's probability of ≥0.01" in the 1-, 3- or 6-hour period ending at the selected time. Alerts: NWS (checked live when possible). AirNow: latest hourly PM2.5 (not the 24-hour AQI). Class I and nonattainment areas: EPA. Perimeters, GACCs, PSAs, ERC/BI: NIFC. Valley inversions and drainage winds can be worse than any grid shows — always get a spot forecast.
